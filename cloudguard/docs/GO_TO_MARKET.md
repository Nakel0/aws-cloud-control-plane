# CloudGuard - Go-to-Market Strategy

## Executive Summary

CloudGuard is a unified AWS cloud optimization platform that addresses three critical pain points for businesses:
1. **Cloud cost waste** (companies waste 30-35% of cloud spend)
2. **Security misconfigurations** (cause of 95% of cloud breaches)
3. **Reliability gaps** (lead to costly downtime)

## Pricing Strategy

### Recommended Model: Per AWS Account

| Tier | Price | Included | Target |
|------|-------|----------|--------|
| **Starter** | Free | 1 account, manual scans | Developers, trials |
| **Team** | $299/mo | 5 accounts, daily scans, basic remediation | Startups |
| **Business** | $799/mo | 20 accounts, hourly scans, full automation | Mid-market |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Unlimited, SSO, SLA, dedicated support | Large orgs |

### Revenue Model Alternatives

1. **% of Savings** - Take 20% of documented savings
   - Pros: Aligned incentives, easy to justify ROI
   - Cons: Revenue unpredictable, hard to scale

2. **Per Resource** - $X per scanned resource
   - Pros: Scales with usage
   - Cons: Complex billing, unpredictable costs

3. **Flat + Usage** - Base fee + per-finding fees
   - Pros: Predictable base, upsell potential
   - Cons: May discourage scanning

**Recommendation**: Start with flat per-account pricing for predictability, consider adding savings-based tier later.

## Sales & Marketing Strategy

### Inbound Marketing

1. **Content Marketing**
   - Blog: AWS cost optimization tips, security best practices
   - Case studies: "How Company X saved $X/month"
   - Comparison guides: CloudGuard vs alternatives

2. **SEO Targets**
   - "AWS cost optimization"
   - "AWS security best practices"
   - "cloud waste reduction"
   - "S3 public bucket scanner"

3. **Developer Relations**
   - Open-source the scanning engine
   - Publish to AWS Marketplace
   - Sponsor cloud/DevOps conferences

### Outbound Sales

1. **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)**
   - AWS spend: $100K+ annually
   - Team size: 50+ employees
   - Industry: SaaS, fintech, healthcare, e-commerce
   - Trigger: Recent AWS bill spike, security audit, compliance requirement

2. **Sales Process**
   - Discovery call (15 min)
   - Free account scan (automated)
   - ROI presentation (show potential savings)
   - Trial period (14 days)
   - Close

3. **Sales Channels**
   - AWS Marketplace (pay with AWS bill)
   - Direct sales (inside sales team)
   - Partner channel (MSPs, consultants)
